# Proposed run packet — `wfh-010`

**Status:** approved direction · not launched

**Authority:** human-approved theme revision and test direction, 2026-08-28

## Exact question

Can the candidate agentic-organization vocabulary express one completed research mission and one
completed software-delivery mission while preserving their different authority, evidence, communication,
adaptation, and learning semantics?

## Hypothesis under test

Use `product/factory/proposals/agentic-organization-vocabulary-hypothesis.md` as the candidate model and
evaluation contract. Test the nine nouns, eight lifecycle verbs, autonomy tiers, authority envelopes,
signal classes, Record envelope, and the unresolved placement of `lesson`.

## Cases

- **Research case:** `wfh-008`, including its distributed workstreams, coverage rework, independent audit,
  synthesis, goal-owner challenge, directional position, and unchanged firewall grades.
- **Software-delivery case:** one completed Unimatrix feature with delegated implementation,
  deterministic tests, review, an effect-bearing merge, and at least one correction or failed gate.
  Selection is part of scope preparation, but the final scope must name one exact feature and its artifact
  paths before the scope gate.

## Confidence and proof bar

- **Confidence:** directional.
- The run may retain, revise, or reject conceptual vocabulary and produce a position.
- It may not claim runtime enforcement, authorize implementation, ratify Jurati's boundary, or move any
  capability or technology to `partial` or `proven`.
- A successful mapping demonstrates conceptual fit across two cases only; it does not establish universal
  domain portability.

## In boundary

- Reconstruct each completed case from durable artifacts rather than memory or invented examples.
- Map all material objects, transitions, actors, delegations, communications, effects, gates, evidence,
  outcomes, lessons, and redirections.
- Compare actual decision latitude with proposed autonomy tiers.
- Express actual effect boundaries as authority envelopes.
- Separate actor disclosure from mechanically captured observation.
- Test Record as a common envelope over extensible Unimatrix categories.
- Rule explicitly on `lesson` as signal, record category, derived construct, multiple roles, or rejected
  primitive.
- Replay one direction change and one false-success/failure path per the hypothesis document.
- Maintain a residue ledger; strained mappings count as evidence against the model.

## Out of boundary

- Product architecture, service boundaries, data-store selection, UI/canvas design, or Jurati design.
- Implementation, schema/configuration changes, new Unimatrix categories, or workflow changes.
- A broad literature or product landscape scan.
- Re-synthesizing `wfh-009` or performing additional MetaHarness research.
- Treating the present garage vocabulary as correct merely because it already exists.
- Claiming that two-domain fit proves open-ended applicability.

## Proposed workstreams

1. **Research-domain mapping.** Map `wfh-008` without redesigning it; produce the complete vocabulary,
   autonomy, authority, signal, Record/category, interruption, and false-success traces.
2. **Software-domain mapping.** Select and name the exact completed feature at scope time, then perform
   the same mapping using its implementation, validation, review, correction, and merge artifacts.
3. **Adversarial comparison.** Attempt to break compression and semantic preservation across both maps;
   own the residue ledger and test whether autonomy, authority, and evidence remain independent.
4. **Vocabulary adjudication.** Compare both mappings and issue `retain | revise | reject` rulings for
   every candidate noun, verb, autonomy tier, signal class, Record semantics, and `lesson` placement.

Workstreams 1 and 2 may run independently. Workstream 3 must be authored independently of both domain
mappings. Workstream 4 occurs only after coverage is accepted and belongs to synthesis/curation under the
canonical protocol.

## Coverage rule

Coverage is ledger-based rather than search-count-based:

1. Every material case artifact is accounted for in an explicit source alphabet.
2. Every material native term maps cleanly, maps with recorded strain, or remains an unclassified residue.
3. Every candidate vocabulary element receives evidence from both domains or is marked unused.
4. Every actual actor receives an autonomy-tier and authority-envelope mapping.
5. Every consequential communication is classified, with disclosure and observation kept distinct.
6. Every durable artifact receives a Record/category ruling.
7. The direction-change and false-success replays are complete in both domains.
8. An independent coverage auditor who authored neither domain mapping rules `PASS`, `REWORKABLE`, or
   `SCOPE-FAIL`. Rework is bounded to two rounds.

The leader proposes sufficiency; the human confirms at the synthesis gate.
