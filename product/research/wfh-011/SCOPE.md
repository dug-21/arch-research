## The question

Can the published Organizational Data Model V5 represent completed research-garage `wfh-008` and
Unimatrix software-delivery `vnc-045` as conforming instances, while rejecting adversarial invalid
instances, without adding domain-specific common constructs or weakening authority, custody, evidence,
effect, interruption, or proof semantics? This is a test of the model by consumption: pressure is logged
and the seven-entity hypothesis is retained, revised, or rejected, but no successor model is authored.

> Can V5 be instantiated and mechanically challenged across both cases as published, with every modeled rule exercised or explicitly left OPEN, rather than improved by interpretation?

## Why it matters

`wfh-010` falsified the original flat-vocabulary hypothesis and produced a structurally coherent V5 with
seven core entities, eight supporting definitions, registered extensions, explicit custody, and typed
separations. That model is still directional and has never consumed one instance. A second review would
repeat argument; instantiation plus deliberately invalid counterexamples is the smallest test that can
show whether the proposed compression carries behavior or merely moves domain meaning into prose.

The run may be scoped, but no instantiation workstream may launch against the current V5 file.
`codex-organizational-data-model-v4-review.md` sanity check S1 demonstrates that V5 is syntactically

Silently quoting, normalizing, patching in memory, or treating the review as an overlay would violate the
fixed-model premise and make a passing result non-reproducible. Repairing the model is outside this
scope. Resume requires all of the following, performed and reviewed outside `wfh-011`:

1. the six scalar defects are corrected in the canonical V5 file, with a whole-file scalar-integrity
   check rather than six assumed spot fixes;

   pass (targets, inverses, references, seed shape, invariant binding, extension ownership);
3. the canonical path and content digest are recorded in an append-only scope extension; and
4. the human gate explicitly resumes Issue #70 and the cycle from this premise hold.

The remaining scope is the approved conditional execution plan. It must not be interpreted as authority
to begin while the hold is active.

## Instance and rejection matrices

Every conforming instance row records: source id; model path; instance id/version; value; provenance;
custody; applicable invariant/check ids; enforcement reality; and any OPEN/pressure disposition. Every
invalid row records: invalid fixture id; model path; mutation from a conforming instance; violated rule;
expected rejection point; actual result; mechanical-versus-judgment classification; and evidence path.

A rule is not counted as tested by paraphrase. It needs at least one conforming witness and, where a
falsifiable invalid instance can be constructed from the alphabets, at least one rejecting counterexample.
If no executable checker exists, the row must say `specified-not-enforced`; a human finding may assess
semantic invalidity but may not report a runtime refusal. Excluded constructs require a negative test
showing the instance does not depend on them or a pressure entry explaining why it does.

## Proof bar

- **Directional, structure-only, no status change.** A pass means the pinned V5 can represent these two
  histories and its stated checks can discriminate the exercised invalid cases at the documented
  mechanical or judgment altitude. It does not prove generality, runtime enforcement, organizational
  effectiveness, a product boundary, or semantic compression outside these cases.
- The seven-entity hypothesis is **retained** only if both cases instantiate without a domain-specific
  common construct, every material semantic fits typed structure or a conforming registered extension,
  and no authority/custody/evidence/effect/interruption/proof distinction weakens.
- It is **revised** if a construct or rule needs changed semantics, identity, lifecycle, or relations but
  the seven-entity boundary remains viable; it is **rejected** if a material case behavior requires an
  eighth common core entity or a current core entity collapses independently behaving concepts.
- A checker accepting an invalid instance, a model rule that cannot identify its subject or rejecting
  point, or a pressure hidden in free text is adverse evidence. Missing enforcement is reported as
  missing enforcement, never converted into model failure or claimed refusal.

## Review / adversarial concern set

1. Did the consumer parse exactly the pinned canonical file, or import intended prose from the review?
2. Does every purported common semantic exist in a typed field/relation/rule rather than instance prose?
3. Are registry/catalog extensions genuinely conforming, program-owned extensions rather than disguised
   new common entities or weakened invariants?
4. Do actor-authored reports remain claims/inferences, and do repository/test/forge observations retain
   their actual provenance and evidence altitude?
5. Are Unit/Attempt retry, rework, interruption, adaptation, baseline, residue, and resume separable?
6. Can a Delegation be traced to a Scope root, compared with its parent, and connected to an independent
   EffectBoundary disposition without treating autonomy as authority?
7. Do gate assessment, gate outcome, decision, transition, effect request/disposition, and Record remain
   distinct and traversable?
8. Do required one-way traversals work without method-biased inverse relations or silent global scans?
9. Were OPEN items exercised and honestly retained, or normalized into convenient defaults?
10. Did the invalid suite test every applicable rule and record absent enforcement as absence?
11. Does `lesson_vs_pattern` remain an evidence question about category behavior rather than a naming
    preference, consistent with Unimatrix's configurable categories?
12. Is the verdict limited to two owner-operated software/research histories and directional evidence?

## Coverage / done call

Coverage is finite and ledger-based:

1. Every M01–M06, R01–R06, and S01–S06 item is `used`, `inspected-no-material-instance`, or
   `unavailable` with reason; every transitive source is added before use.
2. Every X item—each core/supporting key, registry/catalog/value and member, principle/notation rule,
   I1–I19, inverse, extension/ownership/custody rule, exclusion, OPEN item, S1–S8 concern, and required
   traversal—has one reconciled disposition in the construct-pressure ledger.
3. Every applicable X item has a conforming witness in each case where the history supplies one, or an
   explicit `not-applicable`, `blocked-by-OPEN`, or `missing-history` ruling. Absence is never filled.
4. Every applicable falsifiable rule has a minimal invalid fixture and expected rejecting check. Actual
   results distinguish `rejected-mechanically`, `rejected-by-independent-judgment`, `accepted-defect`,
   `specified-not-enforced`, and `not-falsifiable-from-alphabet`.
5. All registries, catalogs, seeded values, and program-owned extension points are exercised in at least
   one case or explicitly dispositioned; empty seeded registries do not receive invented canonical values.
6. The required Goal→Workflow and Actor→Attempt traversals and every semantically declared inverse are
   demonstrated or recorded as a blocking/query-layer hole.
7. W4 reconciles every W1–W3 row, exercises/dispositions every OPEN item, and returns retain/revise/reject
   without drafting a replacement model.
8. An independent coverage auditor rules `PASS`, `REWORKABLE`, or `SCOPE-FAIL`. `REWORKABLE` permits at
   most two targeted rounds; any unreconciled X row, silent model normalization, missing applicable
   rejection, or unresolved premise mismatch after round two is `SCOPE-FAIL`.

The leader proposes sufficiency from the matrices and audit. Human confirmation is required before
synthesis. While the premise gate remains open, the only valid done call is **HOLD-PREMISE**.
