*Rationale:* every other evidence vocabulary in the scan is *enumerated*; only this one is **ordered** — with a **hard floor** (*"inquiry alone does not provide sufficient evidence"*) and a contradiction rule. ### BUILD — 1

**B-1 · Write-time gate-event instrumentation.**

*Mechanism:* every owner gate is a phase boundary; `context_cycle` already emits phase-boundary records with outcomes. Extend the record, **at presentation time rather than reconstruction time**, to carry a **typed disposition** (approved / denied / modified / expired / escalated) · **consequence class** (bootstrapped coarse from the four-way taxonomy) · **elapsed owner time** · the **pre-gate declared intent** (so a near miss is a *diff*) · and a field distinguishing **owner approved** from **owner did not object**.

**Required field — the specific thing assembly cannot deliver:** **a record that distinguishes an owner's approval from an owner's silence, and a record of a refusal that was never made.** No product in the scan produces either for this estate. Denials do not exist as artifacts here — every refusal today is conversational. And these are **unbackfillable**: a naive reconstruction counts every action the owner never saw as a success, systematically, in the agent's favour, forever — **directly corrupting the twenty-run counter §5.7 specifies.** Records tagged coarse can be re-binned; untagged records can never be binned at all.

*Why this and nothing else:* it is the only proposal in ~147 that **survives all five of H-E's own attacks on Cluster G** — by H-E's explicit concession — because its value does not depend on which constraint binds.

*Proposed `done_when`:* for one closed garage run, every owner gate emits a record carrying typed disposition, consequence class, elapsed owner time, declared pre-gate intent, and an explicit approved-versus-unobjected distinction; **and** an independent backfill attempt from Issue history alone recovers dispositions for fewer than a stated fraction of gates — the recovered/unrecovered split being the artifact. **If backfill succeeds, the build is unnecessary and should be abandoned on the spot.**

*The honest caveat:* worst failure mode is Cluster H — a record nobody reads. **At least one required check must consume the record from day one**, or B-1 becomes instance #7 of the pattern it exists to detect.
