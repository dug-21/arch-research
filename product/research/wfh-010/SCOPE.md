## The question

Can the candidate nine nouns, eight lifecycle verbs, autonomy tiers, authority-envelope semantics,
signal classes, Record envelope, and candidate `lesson` concept faithfully express two unlike completed
missions—research-garage `wfh-008` and Unimatrix software-delivery feature `vnc-045`—without flattening
their different authority, evidence, communication, adaptation, learning, and proof semantics? The test
uses only their durable histories and treats strained mappings as evidence against the hypothesis.

> Can one smaller common vocabulary preserve what materially happened in both `wfh-008` and `vnc-045`, including redirection and false-success resistance, without domain-specific exceptions in the common layer?

## Why it matters

The revised workflow-harness theme targets a distributed agentic organization, not a coding-agent harness
or a predetermined Jurati boundary. Standard vocabulary and tiered autonomy could make delegation and
cross-program learning manageable, but cosmetic renaming would instead hide distinct proof bars and effect
authority. A two-domain transfer test is the smallest real check before decomposition: the garage tests
directional knowledge formation, while `vnc-045` tests delegated construction, deterministic verification,
review correction, and an effect-bearing merge.

## Review / adversarial concern set

The independent auditor and synthesis gate must answer these concerns explicitly:

1. **Cosmetic compression:** did the common terms reduce vocabulary, or merely rename native objects while
   relocating meaning into prose?
2. **Proof collapse:** can a reader still distinguish directional world-claims from behavior demonstrated
   by deterministic software tests and merge effects?
3. **Axis aliasing:** do autonomy, effect authority, and evidence produce genuinely independent mappings,
   or is one scale proxying for another?
4. **Tier concealment:** does one autonomy tier hide materially incomparable judgment latitude in the two
   domains or require unsafe exceptions?
5. **Envelope leakage:** does the common envelope remain structural, or become a container for arbitrary
   domain permission lists?
6. **Attribution laundering:** was an agent-authored report, self-declared identity, or source claim ever
   upgraded to observation or attested evidence?
7. **Central-context pressure:** does any signal route require globally sharing local context merely to
   preserve awareness?
8. **Record flattening:** do category-specific admission, provenance, retrieval, and lifecycle semantics
   survive the Record abstraction?
9. **Lesson prejudice:** was `lesson` retained because continual learning sounds important, or rejected
   because it overlaps another noun, without testing distinct behavior, admission, retrieval, reuse, and
   adaptation consequences?
10. **Historical redesign:** did either mapper silently improve the case instead of representing what
    actually happened?
11. **Interruption safety:** does redirection identify who decides, which units stop, what remains durable,
    and the smallest safe resume point?
12. **Selection bias / universality:** is the final claim limited to two owner-operated repositories and
    two workflows, with no implication that open-ended domain portability is established?

### Extended question and provisional Capability rule

Can the recurrent qualifiers exposed by W1–W3 be expressed as a coherent common ontology/data model—rather
than recurring prose—without collapsing program-specific workflow, proof, admission, and category
semantics?

For this round, preserve **Capability** provisionally if and only if one intended meaning remains consistent
at every organizational level. The candidate meaning is:

> **Capability:** an observable behavior required for a goal and delivered or enabled by features and/or
> technologies.

Organizational level may change a Capability's scope or composition, but not its semantics. Actor
competence is a **qualification/skill**, not a Capability. W4 must test this rule against both historical
cases and may recommend `retain | revise | reject`; it must not preserve Capability merely by introducing
level-specific meanings.

### W4 — common ontology/data-model analysis

Assign one `factory-architect` who authored none of W1–W3 to perform a targeted, read-only conceptual
modeling round using the existing W1–W3 ledgers, residues, replays, and fixed R/S alphabets. No historical
case is to be redesigned or rerun.

**Output:** `product/research/wfh-010/findings-W4-data-model.md`

W4 must provide:

1. A minimal identity model for **Capability, Unit, Record, Signal, and Admission Contract**, including
   which have durable identity, how identity survives versioning/supersession, and which are values,
   events, relations, or entities rather than assumed nouns.
2. A relationship/cardinality model connecting goals, Capabilities, features, technologies, Units,

   custody/independence, provenance, attempt/baseline, currentness/supersession, and effect receipt explicit

3. A **type-versus-scope** analysis for each of Capability, Unit, Record, Signal, and Admission Contract:

5. A concrete cross-case representation showing how the proposed model distinguishes research claims and
   admission from demonstrated software behavior and repository effects, while preserving interruption,
   rework, and safe-resume lineage.
6. A delta against W3's nine recurrent qualifiers: identify which become typed fields/relations, which
   remain native workflow/category semantics, and which remain unresolved. Report whether semantic
   compression becomes plausible; do not claim it proven.
7. A residue and contradiction ledger. Any incompatible Capability meaning, identity ambiguity, hidden
   domain exception, or Record/Signal/admission collapse remains a falsifier rather than being normalized
