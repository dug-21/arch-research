# wfh-010 W1 — Research-domain reconstruction and vocabulary mapping

**Role:** `factory-researcher` · **case:** completed directional run `wfh-008` · **method:** read-only reconstruction from alphabet R; no historical workflow executed, no graph write, no grade movement.  
**Evidence convention:** **mechanical observation** means git/forge state captured independently of an actor's prose. **Actor disclosure** means an authored scope, finding, audit, report, comment, or handoff; it remains a claim even when reviewed. “Admission” names the historical domain rule that allowed a disclosure to become durable/accepted, not proof that its world-claim is true.

## 0. Directional finding

The candidate vocabulary can describe most of `wfh-008`, but only if it remains an envelope over—not a replacement for—the garage's native proof, role, gate, and category semantics. The nine nouns cover the case with four strains: **Capability** had no instantiated target; **Unit** is too coarse unless it carries unit kind/dependencies; **Envelope** describes the intended delegation but does not prove custody or enforcement; and **Record** cannot flatten category-specific admission and lifecycle. The verb chain is not a lifecycle as written: `observe` was rarely available, `verify` had at least three meanings, and `learn`/`adapt` occurred iteratively before execution ended.

`lesson` has evidence for a distinct durable category and for a derived learning construct, but not for a distinct transport class in this case. The strongest lesson-like result was the reusable method rule “a ledger naming its blind spot is more trustworthy than one reporting closure”; it arose from audit and correction, could be signalled as a `finding`, and has different retrieval/admission semantics from the target-specific MetaHarness findings. Verdict for W1: **multiple roles only if “lesson” means derived construct + durable category; reject it as a primitive signal class.**

### W1 verdict on `lesson`

- **Signal:** reject. `finding`, `surprise`, `exception`, `directive`, and `decision` already transport every lesson-like event.
- **Record category:** retain as plausible and behaviorally distinct when its purpose is reusable error/process avoidance, its admission requires a witnessed failure/correction or reviewed experience, and retrieval is task-contextual across programs. This case does not justify storing every insight as a lesson.
- **Derived construct:** retain. A lesson is produced by transforming failure/observation/finding plus corrective outcome into reusable guidance.
- **Multiple roles:** yes—derived construct and durable category, not signal.
- **Lifecycle consequence:** a proposed lesson should remain a claim until an independent admission rule accepts it; it may later be corrected/superseded. Reuse should influence future Unit formation/envelopes/procedures, otherwise it is inert information capture rather than organizational learning.

### Unclassified residue

1. **Admission** is load-bearing but currently expressed only by Gate/workflow derivation plus decision Signal and Record metadata. If repeated cross-domain mapping is equally verbose, it may deserve a first-class relation/semantic, not necessarily a noun.
2. **Custody/independence** cannot be inferred from Actor, tier or Envelope. It requires explicit relations between governed party, input producer, credential/effect holder, caller set and assessor.
3. **Attempt/lineage** is necessary for two reworks, corrected reports and replacement leadership. It can remain Unit/Record structure, but cannot remain prose.
4. **Hold/stop/resume** are not candidate verbs. They can be workflow transitions, but interruption safety requires them to be standardized consequences.
5. **Evidence procedure/kind** remains domain-specific. Reuse Unimatrix `#270` only as a defeasible ordering vocabulary; it must not compute truth. Evidence-bound decision evaluation (`#256`) supports typed gates and deterministic next actions, but `wfh-008` itself did not demonstrate that machinery.
