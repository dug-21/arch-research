# wfh-010 W3 — Independent adversarial transfer and compression test

**Role:** `factory-researcher` (independent of W1/W2)  
**Inputs:** completed `findings-W1.md` and `findings-W2.md`, reconciled against canonical `SCOPE.md`  
**Posture:** directional falsification; no synthesis, vocabulary v2, graph write, grade movement, implementation, or build recommendation

## 0. Adversarial verdict

**H1 is revised, not retained as written.** The proposed terms provide useful cross-domain handles, and
the three governance axes remain independently necessary, but the literal claim—nine nouns, eight
lifecycle verbs, tiers, and a flat signal taxonomy expressing both cases without common-layer
exceptions—fails its own falsifiers.

The candidate labels compress a deliberately normalized union of **63 native semantic labels to 31
candidate labels** (9 nouns + 8 verbs + 6 tiers + 8 signal classes), a nominal reduction of **32 labels
(50.8%)**. Excluding tiers, which classify actors rather than replace native object/transition/signal
terms, the comparison is **63 → 25 (60.3%)**. This is real lexical compression but not yet semantic
compression: at least **nine recurrent structural qualifiers** must be promoted from prose—unit kind and
dependency, attempt/baseline, decision authority, admission bar, evidence provenance, effect class,
custody/independence, currentness/supersession, and category lifecycle/retrieval. Without them, more than
three material concepts in each domain require prose exceptions.

The viable hypothesis is narrower: a small **typed organizational envelope** may span both cases while
programs retain their workflows, categories, admission rules, and proof bars. This test does not establish
that such an envelope is complete beyond two owner-operated repositories.

`lesson` survives adversarially as a **derived reusable-guidance construct** and may justify a distinct
durable category when its admission, retrieval, reuse, correction, and adaptation behavior differs from
`finding` or `pattern`. It fails as an independent signal class. The two cases do not settle whether
`lesson-learned` and `pattern` need separate categories.

The axes do not alias. The counterexample is decisive: an A0 service has greater effect authority than an
A2 coordinator, while an A0 reviewer can have workflow consequence without credentials. Evidence grade
never grants initiative or effects. **Retain all three axes.** Do not use tiers as trust, permission, or
evidence shorthand.

### Authority-envelope leakage test

Both maps can express authority structurally using scope/resource/effect/custody/escalation fields, so the
listed “domain permission list required” falsifier is absent. However, the proposed YAML's
`reversible/irreversible` booleans are insufficient: W1 needs graph-write/grade/custody constraints and W2
needs repository/DB/merge effect classes. If these become arbitrary strings interpreted only by each
prompt, leakage is present. A common envelope therefore needs typed effect classes, resource identifiers,
custodian, grant source, expiry/baseline, and escalation triggers; program policy may map those types to
native permissions.

## 6. Proof and admission preservation

The two proof bars remain distinguishable only when `Record` carries or references:

- category and schema version;
- claim/evidence kind, including `reported-observation` versus directly captured observation;
- identified artifact/baseline and content digest/version;
- admission procedure, assessor, independence/custody, verdict, and consequence;
- evidence references and explicit missingness;
- grade/altitude independent of lifecycle status;
- currentness, supersession, correction, and deprecation state.

W1 admitted source-bounded directional findings after coverage audit, curator judgment, goal-owner
challenge, and human acceptance; it moved no proof grade. W2 admitted bounded implementation behavior
through tests and reviews and admitted a repository effect through merge. Neither permits “Record exists”
to mean “claim true.” Prior entry #270's evidence-procedure ordering may route evaluation but cannot
compute truth; #256's evidence-bound decision contract is the closer structural fit.

**Disclosure laundering:** absent in the accepted outcomes. W1/W2 consistently keep authored reports and
self-declared identity as claims. W2's 6,961/32 figures are reported observations because raw logs were not
replayed. Git objects and forge merge metadata establish occurrence/effect, not semantic correctness.

## 7. Signal and central-context test

The histories do not require global sharing of local thought. Researchers and implementers publish scoped
Records; only gate exceptions, premise threats, decisions, directives, and reusable learning cross their
local boundary. A replacement leader recovered from durable mission state rather than every private
context. Thus central-context pressure is **absent** for these cases.

The flat signal taxonomy should nevertheless be revised into orthogonal attributes:

- **epistemic kind:** observation | claim | reviewed finding;
- **speech/action kind:** request | decision | directive;
- **significance:** routine | exception | surprise;
- **route and authority:** sender, recipients/topic, decision authority, required acknowledgement;
- **carrier:** inline payload or Record reference.

This prevents `finding + surprise` and `decision + directive` from appearing as competing mutually
exclusive classes. It also preserves the distinction between a signal and the durable Record carrying it.

## 8. Record/category semantics

`Record` is useful only as an envelope. The cases require different category behavior:

| Semantic | Research requirement | Software requirement | Common ruling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admission | independent coverage + curator/human gates | design/code/test/security/product gates + forge merge | category/workflow-owned; referenced by envelope |
| Provenance | source pin, authorship, evidence labels, commit ordering | baseline commit, diff, test/review target, forge metadata | required common fields |
| Retrieval | technology/finding/position/lesson queries | feature/design/ADR/test/pattern queries | category-specific indexes/routing |
| Lifecycle | correction lineage, claimed grade, retained rejection | active/deferred/stale, rework, merge, audit missingness | currentness and supersession required |
| Effect relation | graph write is separate from report | mutation/commit/merge separate from their records | effect receipt/reference required; no assumed atomicity |

Unimatrix's extensible categories fit this model. The common layer must not enumerate a closed category
ontology. Category is behavior-bearing configuration, not a decorative tag.

## 9. `lesson` alternatives

| Placement | Cross-case test | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| Signal | Every occurrence routes adequately as finding, surprise, exception, decision, directive, or Record reference; no unique addressing/consequence appears. | **reject** |
| Durable Record category | Both cases contain reusable, actionable, non-ADR guidance with plausible task-triggered retrieval and correction/deprecation needs. Distinction from `pattern` is not behaviorally compared. | **insufficient evidence / retain as extension candidate** |
| Derived construct | Both cases show failure or correction transformed into generalized future guidance; not every local correction qualifies. | **retain** |
| Multiple roles | Derived guidance can be stored under a lesson category and later transported, but transport does not make `lesson` a signal kind. | **revise:** derived construct + optional category, never independent signal |
| No primitive | Existing records preserve local corrections, but without an explicit derivation/admission operation continual process improvement becomes implicit. | **reject at construct level**; still possible at category level if `pattern` proves behaviorally identical |

Admission should require a source event/finding, generalized actionable guidance, a stated reuse scope, and
review appropriate to consequence. Retrieval must surface it for analogous future work; reuse outcomes
should permit correction or deprecation. This makes process learning first-class without calling every
failure a lesson.

## 15. Surprises

- The strongest cross-domain commonality is not a noun; it is the **admission contract** joining evidence,
  independent judgment, authority, consequence, and durable state.
- A0 is the tier with the widest authority variance: powerless advisor, blocking validator by workflow,
  credentialed forge, and mutating runtime service. This falsifies any intuitive “higher tier = more
  power” reading.
- `Record` is actively hazardous without currentness: missing cycle events and a stale pre-reduction
  vision report can both look authoritative merely because they are durable.
- Both false-success defenses are primarily social/procedural custody backed by durable evidence, not a
  property of the vocabulary. The vocabulary can expose that custody; it cannot create it.
- The nominal compression is substantial, yet the proposed arrow contributes negative value by implying
  a linear lifecycle that neither case followed.
