## 3. Failure patterns

**FP-1 — The declared-but-unenforced boundary.** (P-01,02,03,04,12,13,19,30) A constraint is written in a document the agent reads; compliance is the model's; **no external check runs at the moment of the constrained action**; the violation surfaces later, when a human reads an artifact — or never. #179 states the general case. The instance worth naming: **#179 was produced by wfh-002 days before wfh-002 closed on exactly the failure #179 describes.** The run diagnosed its own cause and the diagnosis changed nothing — because a finding in a graph is also just prose.

**FP-2 — Crossing a session boundary de-addresses the work.** (P-05,06,09,10,29) The parent retains only what the child returns as text; the association between work and run/role does not survive the hop. Loss modes: telemetry (P-05), attribution (P-06), capability (P-09 — a role instantiated without its own contract's tools), liveness (P-10), and output persistence (P-29).

**FP-3 — The confident wrong number.** (P-05,12,14,16,25) A report is emitted, looks internally consistent, is wrong, and the error is found only by building an independent instrument or hand-diffing two documents.

**FP-4 — The compensating control of record is the owner's attention.** In every incident where cost was actually contained, the containing agent was a person: P-01 (owner flagged it), P-03 (owner-directed close), P-10 (manual nudge), P-16 (POC built on suspicion), P-22 (manual `/mcp`), P-27/P-28 (owner supplied the correction). This is the demand signal restated: each row above marks a place where autonomy is currently backstopped by a human.

### The §3.2 layering claim — evidence for AND against

**For.** P-05 is decisive and cleaner than SCOPE §3.2 states it: the instrumentation was *present and working* — `settings.json` carries a `SubagentStart` hook **[V]**, and the same telemetry produced 186 records for shd-004. It still reported 0, purely because subagent sessions were not named units of the cycle. Measurement fine, addressing missing. P-06 extends it: `agent_id` discarded → no write attributable to a role → the single-writer *security* rule is unauditable, exactly the diagram's `Structure → Introspection → Security assurance` path. P-11 is the same shape in the file/graph seam.

**Against — four counter-examples the claim should not absorb.**

1. **P-16 breaks "everything else is an attribution problem."** The 61× cost undercount had *nothing* to do with missing structure. Attribution by `cwd` worked; the tokens *were* addressed to the right repo. The number was wrong because the parser summed `input+output` and ignored `cache_read` (86.5% of tokens). **Metric correctness is an axis independent of addressing** — structure was sufficient and the measurement was wrong anyway.
2. **P-15 inverts the arrow for cost.** Subscription quota is observable at *no* granularity — *"exposed via none of: env vars · HTTP headers · local files · OTEL export · CLI --json."* No amount of internal structure produces a quantity the provider does not emit. Here Cost management is blocked **upstream** of structure, not downstream of it.
3. **P-04 over-claims for security.** A `PreToolUse` deny on `Bash(git add -A:*)` needs no named units, no roles, no addressing — it is a pattern match on a tool call. We have zero. So `Structure → Security enforcement` holds for *role-scoped* enforcement and not for *action-scoped* enforcement — and action-scoped is the cheap half we also never did.
4. **P-10/P-22/P-23 split Recovery in two.** Detecting that a pipe stopped producing bytes needs a liveness signal, not an addressing scheme. You can detect a dead process without knowing whose it is; you cannot *resume the right unit* without knowing. The diagram's wording (`Recovery (resume a named unit)`) is right; SCOPE §3.1's definition of Recovery (which includes *"is detected"*) is not fully downstream of Structure. P-20 makes the same point: the units were named and it did not help.

**Net restatement offered to triage:** *structure is the addressing scheme; abilities that must say **which** are downstream of it — abilities that must say **whether** are not.* Attribution, resumption, security *assurance*, self-improvement, per-unit cost → downstream. Liveness detection, action-scoped enforcement, provider-side emission, metric correctness → not downstream. Triage's §3.2 cost-to-prove rider should apply only to the first list.

## 5. Unmapped pains / candidate emergent concerns

**E-1 — Commitment / obligation tracking.** *"The harness holds a run to what it declared."* Populated by **five** distinct pains: P-19 (retro declared deferred, never performed, obligation evaporated), P-12 (gate authorized 17 nodes, 33 written, never reconciled), P-13 (a declared role never ran; the run reported its funnel anyway), P-03 (a declared out-of-scope boundary not held), P-26 (five runs declared in progress, open a month, no state distinguishing blocked from abandoned). §3.3 test: **(1) property of operation** — yes, stated behaviorally. **(2) irreducible** — arguably: Structure gives named units, Introspection reports what happened, Human steering provides gate points, Recovery covers *failure*; none of them says *a declaration made at t0 is checked against reality at t1*. **(3) ≥2 pains** — five. **Counter-case, stated honestly:** a maximalist reading of Recovery's *"never silently abandoned"* absorbs P-19/P-26, and Structure+Introspection together could be argued to yield the detection. Researcher's read: the reduction is available but strained, and the five pains are the register's most expensive rows (a lost run, a 3× overrun, a skipped stage, a dropped retro, a month of stalls). **Recommend admitting it to the next round's frame per the two-tier rule.** Layering placement: downstream of Structure (a declaration must name what it binds), parallel to Introspection.

**E-2 — Calibration / trustworthiness of self-measurement.** *"The numbers the harness reports about itself are checkable against something."* Populated by P-05 (0 vs 38), P-16 (61×), P-25 (a write reported success while dropping edges), P-14 (a report contradicting itself), P-12. §3.3 test: **(1)** yes. **(2) irreducible — weak.** Introspection as defined ("what is happening now, what happened, and why") covers *producing* the record; it is silent on the record being *correct*. But the honest counter is that this is §3.1's own evidence-discipline gate applied to telemetry rather than to research claims — i.e. it may be *Evidence discipline*, which §3.1 has provisionally filed under Structure. **(3)** five pains. **Recommend: do not promote as its own concern; instead treat it as the second population for Evidence discipline**, which §3.1 explicitly says to promote at ≥2 non-reducible members. P-16 and P-25 are exactly that: evidence discipline applied to the harness's own instruments rather than to research output.

**E-3 — Continuity across the boundaries of an agent's own existence (borderline, reported as NOT promotable).** P-20 (4/4 runs; 617-min gap → 12 re-reads), P-21, P-29. Neither seeded concern names *time* as the variable: Recovery is scoped to failure, and nothing failed in a 40.7h planned gap; Context provisioning covers what is injected but not what should have persisted. **However**, the observable pain — *"the agent re-read 12 files it had already read"* — is precisely "what this agent knows at this step was not decided and injected." **It reduces to Context provisioning.** Filed here so the lenses see the seam, not promoted.

**Not a concern, but out of the frame entirely — flagged so it is not silently lost:** P-15's wall (quota observable at no granularity, pool shared with claude.ai/Cowork) is a **property of the provider, not of any harness we could build**. No candidate under Cost should be routed on the assumption it can be dissolved; per SCOPE §11 discipline, cost candidates depending on it route `needs-a-probe`, never `out`.

## 6. Caveats on this partition's own evidence

- **Recurrence counts are floors, not measurements.** They count what got written down. Per P-05, the more autonomous a run, the less of it is recorded — so recurrence is systematically biased *downward*, most for exactly the failures worth counting.
- **The three retros that never ran (P-19)** mean wfh-002 — the run that closed on a process defect — contributed *no* OBS entries. The register's most recent recorded observation is OBS-14 (2026-07-22); everything from wfh-002 onward in this document is the researcher's own, marked **[I]** or **[V]**.
- **P-08, P-12, P-14, P-26, P-30 were not previously recorded anywhere.** They came out of this sweep. That five new pains surfaced from a two-hour read of existing artifacts is itself a datum about Introspection: the record exists, and nobody was reading it.
- **Nothing here demonstrates a fix works.** Every row is a failure observation. No ability named in the missing-ability column has been shown to prevent its pain — that is generation's problem and triage's screen.
