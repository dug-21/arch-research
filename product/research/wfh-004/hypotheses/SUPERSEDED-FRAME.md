# ⚠ These registers were generated under a superseded frame

**Status:** wrong-framed, retained as a **dedup reference**. See `../SCOPE.md` Amendment **A-7**.

## What was wrong

Every register in this directory was generated against a **single-domain surface**: the incumbent
baseline (`W0-b`) inspected only this repo's *research* configuration, and the pain record (`W0-d`)
catalogued 30 incidents, all from *research* runs.

The run's actual target — clarified by the owner on 2026-07-25 — is **an agentic workflow harness,
considered for build in JURATI, over the LLMs, intended to serve SDLC and research roughly equally**,
with the understanding that the workflow *definitions* differ while the *harness* is one thing.

A single-domain demand signal cannot establish a two-domain requirement. The owner directed a rebuild
rather than a downstream correction (Amendments A-5 → A-6 → **A-7**).

## What these files are still good for

**A dedup reference — not a menu.** Exactly the status `surface/W0-a-landscape-by-concern.md` holds.

The ~330 candidates here paid their Rule-2 mechanism debt from **aviation, nuclear operations, insurance,
epidemiology, power-grid operations, maritime regulation, object-capability security, distributed
systems, military doctrine, manufacturing, medicine, and finance**. Not one of those is a research
discipline. That work is not domain-contaminated and should not be re-derived from scratch.

**Binding on the rebuild:** a mechanism may be re-admitted only if it is **re-stated at the harness layer
against two-domain evidence**. Carrying a candidate forward unchanged is the patch A-7 forbids.

## The layer cut that the rebuild applies and these registers do not (A-6)

- **HARNESS-LAYER** — a property of the engine; holds for any workflow definition running on it.
- **DEFINITION-LAYER** — a property of a *particular* workflow (a specific gate, role vocabulary, phase
  order). Not a defect — **correct content at the wrong altitude**, which converts into a harness
  requirement: *"the harness enforces the curator rule"* becomes *"the harness can express and enforce a
  declared single-writer constraint."*

Most apparent garage-specificity in these files is definition-layer content awaiting that conversion.

## Analytical results worth carrying forward regardless of frame

These are arguments, not candidates, and none of them is research-specific:

- **The C-4 family** — authority that is *necessarily* external: self-attestation is not attestation;
  self-grading is not grading; self-certified completion is not completion; **a dead process cannot report
  its own death.** (L2 §2, reached independently by L1, L3, L4.)
- **The C-3 diff** — four attacks on "controlled comparison is not expressible," resolved into one growth
  path rather than four rivals. (R2-4 §3.)
- **The adoption analysis** — five crossings from incident to verified improvement, with a recorded
  failure at every one, and the mechanical statement: *an improvement is adopted when it becomes
  configuration or enforcement; a knowledge graph can only ever make it context.* (R2-4 §1.)
- **The cost reframe** — the mature answer to unforecastable work is not a better forecast: bound the
  tail, book commitments, tier who may commit, internalize overruns. (R2-1 §1, R2-5 note 1.)
- **The dynamic-shape invariant** — *not "the shape doesn't change" but "the shape and the work never
  change independently."* (R2-3 §3.)
- **The shared-surface caveat (L3-31)** — all six round-1 lenses were handed the same four W0 documents,
  so convergence counts overstate independence. **This applies to the rebuild too** and must be designed
  around, not merely noted.
