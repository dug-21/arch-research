## 2. Transplant notes (the richest borrowings, argued)

**1. Underwriting: the mature answer to pricing unforecastable work is not forecasting (R2-5-01/02/03/04/19).** The Cost cell stayed thin in round 1 partly because every lens hunted a *prediction* ability, and prediction is genuinely hard for heterogeneous work — L1's own note conceded *"almost none predict well."* Insurance is the discipline that made peace with that: **it never predicts an individual outcome.** It **bounds the tail** (PML — arithmetic over declared limits, no history), **books commitments as liabilities the moment they're made** (reserving/encumbrance), **tiers who may commit how much** (binding authority — an authority the committer cannot raise for itself, i.e. C-4-shaped), and **makes the insured bear first losses** (deductible — the moral-hazard control is bearing, not surveillance). Together these convert "we cannot forecast a run's cost" from a blocker into a solved operating posture: **you don't need the forecast if commitments are booked, tails are bounded, and overruns are internalized.** Strain, honestly: insurance works on portfolios large enough for the law of large numbers; the garage's n is tiny — which is why these four are *deterministic bookkeeping and authority structures* (valid at n=1) while experience rating (R2-5-19), the one genuinely statistical borrowing, carries the small-n credibility problem in its falsifier. **Insurance itself names that limit** (credibility theory), and the honest transplant imports the limit with the mechanism.

**2. Screening economics and alarm rationalization: alarm fatigue has arithmetic, not just anecdotes (R2-5-13/14).** Round 1 kept meeting the same tension — detectors are wanted, detectors that fire wrongly get ignored or banned (P-01 is the garage's own alarm banned by its one consumer) — and handled it in falsifier clauses. Two disciplines *govern* it instead. Epidemiology computes, before any screening program launches, what the PPV will be at the population's base rate, and that computation decides whether a positive may trigger treatment (blocking) or only investigation (queue) — the identical decision the harness must make about wiring any detector to a deny. Process industry, after control rooms drowned operators, codified ISA 18.2: an alarm may exist only with a defined response, a priority, and a per-consumer rate budget; alarms failing the test are removed, and the alarm *population* is audited as a system. Strain: base-rate estimation needs labeled incident counts, which needs case definitions (R2-5-16) — **these three rows form a dependency chain that should be stated to triage as such.**

**3. Mission command: the redirection hole is a grammar problem before it is a channel problem (R2-5-29/30/31).** Four sources declared the steering-redirection cell empty and R2-2 was chartered at the *channel*. Mission command solved the other half — what a steer must *contain* — under exactly the harness's constraint: a principal who cannot be consulted at each decision, subordinates who meet circumstances the instruction didn't anticipate. Its three inventions map one-to-one: **intent** travels with every order so the unit can deviate *correctly* (P-27 is a pure missing-intent failure); **CCIR** makes interruptibility a pre-declared content policy rather than moment-by-moment judgment (the garage has *neither* half of the interruption policy); **FRAGO** makes change-in-execution a numbered, acknowledged delta (P-03 is a stale-effective-charter failure). Strain: the doctrine's enforcement is professional culture and after-action accountability, neither of which binds an LLM; the transplant keeps the *deterministic record structures* and concedes the judgment steps to C-2 leaves. **R2-5-29's falsifier is the cheapest and most important test in this register:** it gates whether the expressiveness half of the hole is fillable at all.

**4. Maritime's attestation economy: verification with teeth and no regulator (R2-5-10/11/12).** The multi-tenant "demonstrable to a stranger" step-function needs verification a counterparty can trust without trusting the operator. Shipping — maximally multi-tenant, jurisdictionally fragmented, no global regulator — evolved a three-layer answer: **classification societies** issue standing certificates on the *vessel itself*, with teeth from interlock (no class → no insurance → no cargo) rather than law; **port state control** exists because the layer under it failed — flag states, chosen and paid by owners, raced to the bottom, so the *receiving* jurisdiction inspects un-waivably and pools findings; and the failure that necessitated PSC — **choosing your own certifier** — independently re-occurred in credit ratings, convergent evolution and the Rule-2 evidence for R2-5-12. The garage's miniature is on record: P-13's gate graded its own inputs. Strain, plainly: the whole economy runs on multiple counterparties checking certificates; at single-tenant scale these rows have teeth in nothing — **honest L4-activated candidates**.

**5. The grid under partial information: estimate, ride through, restart from nothing (R2-5-15/23/25).** Round 1's introspection rows assume telemetry that is *incomplete*; the grid's operating assumption is telemetry that is incomplete **and wrong** — the garage's measured reality. Grid operators act on a **state estimator's** best fit across all sensors, whose residuals localize the lying meter — the global-fit generalization of L1-26, and the instrument P-05 lacked. **N-1** inverts recovery from reactive to anticipatory — P-22's MCP dependency was an unassessed single point. **Black start** attacks the orderly-death assumption in L1-07's handover: restoration plans that depend on the failed system's cooperation famously fail. Strain: state estimation leans on redundant, *physically independent* sensors; the garage's sources are fewer and partially derived from one another, so residuals may not localize — L1-19's independence caveat inherited undiminished.

**6. The adoption engine: aviation learns, but agriculture and the Army *distribute* (R2-5-19/20/21/22).** L1's SI rows cover generating and measuring; L3 flagged that nobody covered *uptake*, and the record shows why — P-01's fix was adopted (a memory file written) and had zero uptake. The disciplines that own the last mile: **agricultural extension** measures adoption as a curve and treats zero-uptake as a *translation* failure distinct from a bad idea; **CALL** pushes digested lessons at matched relevance rather than archiving them (**and the garage already owns the injection plumbing, making this one of the register's cheapest rows**); **insurance** propagates practice change by *pricing* non-adoption (sprinklers spread through fire-premium differentials, not fire codes); **deliberate practice** gates adoption on rehearsal. Four fields, four distinct verbs — measure uptake, push at relevance, price non-adoption, rehearse before adoption. Strain: extension and CALL both staff *human translation labor* a one-human garage cannot staff.

## 5. Emergent concerns (§3.3 — no fourth label added)

**E-1 — further corroborated, plus one addition.** Two more independent inventions of the obligation-as-first-class-object: insurance **claims reserving** (an open obligation is a *priced* liability from day one) and triage's **expectant disposition** (non-action must be a recorded decision, never a default). That extends L1's convergent-evolution argument from four fields to six. The addition E-1 should absorb: obligations carry cost-to-discharge (R2-5-33) and an explicit expectant disposition (R2-5-27) — **deferral and abandonment both become priced, recorded verdicts rather than silences.**

**The three competing "E-4" proposals — engaged; this register feeds all three, which is itself the datum:**
- **L2's principal identity & attestation:** the strongest contribution. Maritime independently evolved the exact object — the **IMO number**, a permanent hull identity introduced *because* reflagging and renaming were laundering vessel histories, defeating class records and port-state targeting. **The general law visible across these fields: any mechanism that prices or targets on history (R2-5-19, R2-5-11, R2-5-10) creates the incentive to shed identity, and every field that ran into this invented unforgeable permanent identity in response** (IMO numbers, VINs, tail numbers). So identity is not free-standing — it is *forced* by history-based mechanisms, which argues for L1's original disposition (a capability cluster under Structure, upstream-required) rather than a ninth concern; but the forcing relationship should be recorded, because adopting R2-5-19 or R2-5-11 without identity (P-06) imports the laundering failure on day one.
- **L3's containment/revocability:** populated by R2-5-24 and R2-5-05. Both file cleanly under Recovery/Security; nothing found makes containment irreducible.
- **L4's coordination/contention:** populated by R2-5-07 and R2-5-06. Members supplied, irreducibility not evidenced from this ground.

**Read offered to W6 without deciding:** a register from five deliberately different discipline families supplied members to all three proposals while making none look irreducible — consistent with all three being capability clusters, with L2's identity cluster distinguished by being *upstream-forced* by other candidates rather than merely useful.

**No new concern proposed.** Two near-misses declined: "attention economics" (reduces to Human steering) and "practice/rehearsal" (reduces to Self-improvement).
