# L4 — Scale extrapolation

**Run:** `wfh-004` · Issue #48 · phase `hypothesize` · lens L4 · read-only, zero graph writes.

**Lens:** what the harness must provide when the garage is no longer one owner, one repo, one run, a handful of subagents, and a human watching. Axes worked: fleet size, repo/domain count, tenant count, time, owner count, spawn depth, corpus history — independently and in combination. Seeds: the W0-d pain record extrapolated forward.

**A pattern worth naming up front (feeds A-3):** this lens's candidates are unusually *deterministic*. Scale forces machine-checkability, and machine-checkable things are mostly not inference. Where inference survives in these rows it is genuinely irreducible (semantic anomaly explanation, staleness judgment, cross-domain dedup) — the deterministic shell around it grows with n, the LLM leaf does not.

## 2. The step-function list — where scale changes *kind*, not degree

Highest-value section, per charter. Each: the threshold, and the mechanical reason the kind changes.

1. **Review-by-reading → review-by-exception.** *(~20 concurrent agents.)* Human review is O(n·transcript-length) against fixed attention. The output consumed by oversight must change type: from prose a human reads to metrics/manifests a machine checks with a human clearing exceptions (L4-01, -07, -09). Not "more dashboards" — the *artifact of record* changes from transcript to checkable claim.
2. **Convention → enforcement.** *(~50 constrained actions.)* Arithmetic: prose compliance at 99%/action gives P(violation) ≈ 63% at n=100. A convention *has* a compliance rate; enforcement doesn't. The entire FP-1 class (8 of 30 pains) is this threshold already crossed at n=1 (L4-08).
3. **"Ask the owner" → queue with latency budget and default-on-timeout.** *(Concurrent gate-requests > one human's availability.)* The kind change: silence becomes a decision. At n=1, an unanswered gate means "wait"; at n=100, unbounded waiting is indistinguishable from abandonment (P-26), so every gate class must declare what no-answer means (L4-11, -12).
4. **Isolation believed → isolation demonstrable.** *(Tenant count 1 → 2 — a step at exactly 2.)* The consumer of the security property changes from yourself (trusting) to a stranger (adversarial). Assurance must move from behavioral ("the model was told not to") to structural ("assembly never handed it over, and here is the manifest") — the C-4 shape, and why jurati#12 promotes the verifier to foundational (L4-33, -34, -22).
5. **Retry-forbidden → retry-constant → idempotency mandatory.** *(Fleet + autonomy.)* At n=1 a duplicate side effect is an incident (P-08); at n=1000 with retry-by-default, non-idempotent side effects make duplicates the steady state. Side-effect semantics change from "execute" to "ensure" (L4-06).
6. **A/B impossible → A/B nearly free.** *(~20 runs/arm.)* An *inverted* step-function: W0-b C-3's blockers (sampling noise, corpus mutation) are properties of n=1 that *average out and randomize* across cohorts. The replay substrate the constraint demands at n=1 is unnecessary at fleet. Self-improvement's hardest prerequisite dissolves with scale rather than growing (L4-38; also the sharpest available attack on C-3-as-physics — flag to L2).
7. **Parent-watches-child → supervision as structure.** *(Depth 3.)* At depth 2 the root is one hop from every worker. At depth ≥3 the watcher is itself an unwatched LLM; liveness and authority must propagate structurally (leases, attenuated capabilities) because attention cannot (L4-03, -35, -29).
8. **Wrong number embarrassing → wrong number contractual.** *(Tenant ≥ 2.)* P-16's 61× undercount cost nothing but pride at n=1. Under metered tenancy the same defect is a billing dispute. Metric correctness changes from hygiene to liability, which is what forces double-entry (L4-27, -28).
9. **Anecdote → base rate.** *(~100 executions per failure class.)* "A WebFetch failed 4 times" is a story; at fleet volume the same events are a rate with confidence bounds — evidence *class* changes, and with it what retros, triage, and the firewall can consume (L4-37, and the E-2 discussion).
10. **Human memory as obligation carrier → obligation ledger.** *(Gaps > days; any personnel change.)* P-19's retro obligation evaporated across one session boundary with one very engaged owner. The carrier's half-life is fixed while horizon and headcount grow; commitments must live in something with no half-life (L4-16, -17 — the E-1 core).

## 3. Dissolved-at-scale list — the honest opposite

1. **Per-item human review of routine outputs.** Statistical sampling with confidence bounds (L4-09) replaces exhaustive reading — cheaper *and* epistemically stronger (a coverage figure instead of an unquantified "I looked").
2. **Prevention of transient faults.** P-22 (stale MCP connection, manual `/mcp` ×4) and P-23 (WebFetch ×4) are incidents at n=1. At fleet scale, retry-with-backoff and redundant issuance make individual transient failures non-events; the ability that matters shifts from *preventing* the failure to *knowing its rate* (L4-37). Buying per-call reliability would be over-engineering.
3. **First-principles cost estimation.** At n=1 predicting a run's cost requires reasoning; at ~100 archived runs the empirical distribution beats any model of the work (L4-31). The prediction ability shouldn't be *built*, it should be *harvested*.
4. **Artisanal dedup.** P-11's prose-naming convention for findable candidates dissolves under mechanical identity (content addressing, uniqueness keys — L4-06/-36); nobody should maintain a naming convention at n=1000.
5. **The single-human gate as the default control — flagged honestly as *forced replacement*, not dissolution.** FP-4 shows owner attention is the compensating control in every contained incident. Scale doesn't make that control unnecessary; it makes it *unavailable*. Listed here because triage should not route candidates on the assumption the owner-in-the-loop remains an option — but no one should read this as "the need went away."
6. **Bespoke per-run workflow authoring.** At repo/domain count ≥ ~3, hand-tailored protocols must dissolve into template + bindings (L4-18) — the ability to hand-craft each run's process is one scale removes rather than demands.

## 5. Proposed emergent concerns (§3.3 applied)

**E-1 — commitment/obligation tracking: SUPPORT promotion, with a scale multiplier.** W0-d's five pains hold at n=1 with maximal owner attention. Every scale axis multiplies the declared-vs-actual gap faster than any seeded concern covers it: at 500 runs the obligation set is unknowable without a ledger (L4-10, -16, -17), and at tenant ≥ 2, commitment tracking *externalizes* — an SLA is an obligation ledger a stranger can read. The maximalist-Recovery reduction W0-d honestly offers gets *weaker* at scale: Recovery detects failed work; none of it detects work that was promised and never started. Three L4 candidates populate it independently → passes test 3 comfortably.

**E-2 — calibration: AGREE with W0-d (do not promote; file under evidence discipline), with one addition.** Scale changes its economics: independent measurement paths (provider bill vs internal meter, transcript vs telemetry, authorized vs written) exist *naturally* at fleet/tenant scale, so calibration becomes cheap reconciliation (L4-27) rather than expensive instrument-building. Second population for evidence-discipline promotion per SCOPE §3.1's own rider.

**E-4 (new) — coordination/contention: PROPOSED, with the counter-case stated.** *The ability: concurrent healthy units acting on shared resources never lose, corrupt, or starve work — observable as torn-artifact count, starvation incidents, and abandoned partial batches all zero under concurrency.* Tests: (1) property of operation — yes, stated behaviorally. (2) Irreducible? — the honest crux. Recovery covers *failed* units; Structure covers *addressing*; neither says anything about two *healthy* units interfering (P-24 and P-25 involved no failure and no missing name). The available reduction is "Structure generalized to resource dependencies" — real but strained, same strain grade W0-d assigns E-1's reduction. (3) ≥2 capabilities: L4-04, -05, -20, -41 (four). Layering placement: downstream of Structure (arbitration needs named units and named resources), parallel to Recovery. Per the two-tier rule: recorded for the next round's frame, not this run's grid. Honest caveat: at n=1 it is nearly invisible (2 of 30 pains), which is exactly the profile a scale lens *would* over-promote — triage should weigh that.

**Considered and rejected:** "tenancy/accountability-to-strangers" as a concern — fails test 2; it is a *consumer change* that re-cuts Security + Introspection + Cost (every tenant-triggered candidate above files cleanly under a seeded concern). The step-function (believed → demonstrable) is real but it is a property of who reads the evidence, not a new operational ability class.
