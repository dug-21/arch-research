## 2. Transplant notes (the richest borrowings, argued)

**1. Object-capabilities and the confused deputy (L1-10/11/12/13, the L1-13 core).** The security field's deepest relevant result is that *filtering* malicious instructions is the losing strategy and *rebinding authority* is the winning one. Hardy's confused-deputy paper showed the deputy (a compiler with billing authority) could not be fixed by making it smarter about its inputs — only by making authority travel *with* designation, so the attacker's designation arrives powerless. Every shipped LLM harness is currently a confused deputy by construction: W0-a found zero prompt-injection defenses in ~30 tools, and W0-b C-1 shows the incumbent's representation cannot even express the data/instruction distinction. The transplant does not require solving NLP: it requires that *acting* be gated on possession of tokens the data plane cannot carry. Where it strains: ocap assumes the subject's reference graph is inspectable; an LLM's "possession" is a context window, so the token check must live at the tool boundary (queen-side, per C-1), not inside the model — which is exactly where the incumbent's PreToolUse channel already sits, unused (W0-b §0-ii). This family is also the C-4 hunting ground the constraints file asks for: authority the actor cannot mint for itself.

**2. The sterile cockpit (L1-42).** The FAA did not fix interruption-at-criticality by training or memos — it made a *phase-scoped communication rule* enforceable, after accident analysis showed cockpit discipline collapsed exactly when workload peaked. The structural insight: interaction policy was made a property of the *flight phase*, not of the crew's judgment. P-01 is a three-recurrence demonstration that the garage's current mechanism (a memory file asking the model to behave) is the memo, and the memo fails. The strain to name honestly: aviation's rule silences the *crew*; here we silence the agent's questions toward the human, and a silenced agent may guess instead — the falsifier is real and cheap to test.

**3. Double-entry, reconciliation, trial balance (L1-19/20, feeding E-2).** Accounting's ~700-year-old discovery is that error detection scales when records are produced *twice by independent paths* and compared, rather than inspected harder. P-05 (0 vs 38) and P-16 (61×) are both single-ledger failures: one instrument, no counterparty record, error invisible until an accidental independent derivation appeared. The transplant is precise: the spawner journals what it commissioned (grants, budgets, expected outputs); the spawned unit's stream is the second ledger; imbalance is computed at close. Strain: true independence is the load-bearing part — two views derived from one transcript share failure modes, which is why the note on L1-26 (diverse instruments, nuclear I&C) belongs to the same family.

**4. Supervision trees and default-death polarity (L1-36/37/38).** OTP's insight is architectural humility: workers are not made reliable, *trees* are — death is detected by linkage, restart policy is declared, escalation is structural. This matches LLM agents unusually well (unreliable workers, cheap respawn). The biology twin adds the polarity argument: multicellular organisms prevent runaway components by making *survival* the thing that requires continuous signaling, not death. P-10 (silent death) and P-21 (15% of calls after completion) are the two polarities' failures respectively. The C-4 strain is named in the L1-38 row: the halt must be effected by the supervisor's expiring grant, not by the unit's obedience, or it is prose again.

**5. ASRS + FOQA — the two-channel safety-learning system (L1-31/32).** Aviation learns through *two decoupled channels*: voluntary blame-free narrative reports (ASRS) and mandatory automatic telemetry screening of every flight (FOQA). Neither depends on a crash, and neither depends on the crew's diligence at shift end. The garage's single learning mechanism — the retro — is coupled to run close and was dropped three times (P-19), including by the run that closed *on a process defect*. The transplant argument is strong precisely because aviation tried the garage's design first (post-incident review only) and abandoned it as insufficient. Strain: ASRS's engine is immunity from sanction, and it is unclear what "sanction" means to an LLM agent — the decoupling may matter less for the reporter and more for the *reader* (reports that can't embarrass a run's grade actually get filed and read).

**6. Pre-registration and matched cohorts (L1-33/35).** Medicine's crisis — outcome-switching, publication bias — is structurally identical to P-13/P-18: the entity reporting the result also chooses, after seeing the data, which result to report; and no two runs are replayable (patients, like research runs, cannot be re-run under the other arm). Medicine's two-part answer transplants cleanly: bind the report to a pre-declared endpoint (cheap, near-free in the SCOPE file already), and when true A/B is impossible — which W0-b C-3 argues it is here — use matched cohorts with pre-registered metrics as the honest weaker instrument. This directly attacks C-3's "A/B is not expressible without an external replay substrate" by conceding the replay and weakening the claim: not *proof* of superiority, but *evidence* with declared confounders — which beats the current regime of 16 versions shipped on assertion by an unbounded margin.

**7. Flight strips and the conn (L1-01/44).** Two fields (ATC, naval bridge/aviation) independently converged on the same object: responsibility is a *token held by exactly one principal*, transferred only by explicit mutual acknowledgment, with the full inventory of held tokens visible at a glance. Both fields adopted it after incidents of the P-02/P-08 shape — work performed by a station that didn't hold responsibility, or two stations each assuming the other had it. The board-of-strips is also the answer to W0-d's sharpest finding ("nothing observes the run as an object"): the strip *is* the run as an object. Strain: the garage parallelizes specialists deliberately; the conn must be scoped to coordination authority or it fights the swarm — the row's falsifier.

**8. Conduct of operations and challenge-and-response (L1-05/43).** Nuclear operations and CRM both discovered that self-reported completion is unreliable *under exactly the conditions that matter*, and both externalized confirmation: place-keeping marks made at the moment of performance, verified by a second party against effects; checklists run as challenge-and-response, never self-affirmed. P-12 (17 authorized, 33 written) and P-13 (the gate grading its own inputs) are textbook single-party-confirmation failures. This family is also where C-2 lands gracefully: the constraint says the semantic verdict needs an LLM leaf — conduct-of-ops says the *value* was never in the verdict's brilliance but in the deterministic scaffold around it (who was asked, what shown, forced binary, recorded). The transplant and the constraint agree, which is evidence for both.

## 4. Proposed emergent concerns

**E-1 (commitment/obligation tracking) — endorse promotion, with cross-domain corroboration.** W0-d's three-part test already passes it; this lens adds an independent argument: at least four unrelated mature fields — military correspondence (suspense files), law (docket control), clinical research (AE tracking to disposition), mission ops (open-item/action-item tracking in flight readiness reviews) — each *separately invented* a first-class "declared obligation with deadline, owner, and tracked disposition" object, distinct from their audit trails (Introspection) and their failure handling (Recovery). Convergent evolution across fields is evidence of irreducibility — the same evidence pattern Rule 2 accepts for mechanisms. Populated here by L1-49/50/51 plus spans from L1-05/20/33. Layering placement: downstream of Structure (an obligation binds a named thing), parallel to Introspection, as W0-d proposed.

**E-2 (calibration) — concur with W0-d: do not promote.** File as the second population of evidence discipline. L1-19/20/26 are its capability-level members; nothing this lens found makes it irreducible as a *concern*.

**E-4 (proposed, weakly) — principal identity.** *"Every action is attributable to a distinguishable principal that cannot be impersonated by another, observable as no two actors sharing an identity in any record."* Surfaced because **six** of this register's transplants (L1-14, 16, 21, 44, plus the maker/checker and custody families generally) silently presuppose it, and the incumbent lacks it (P-06: `created_by: anonymous`, 1 month standing; all agents are one principal). Applying §3.3 honestly: (1) property of operation — yes; (2) irreducible — **doubtful**: it is arguably the actor-half of Structure's addressing scheme ("named units" should include named actors), and W0-d already files P-06 under Introspection/Security; (3) ≥2 capabilities — yes (unforgeable identity; identity-persistence across the session hop; identity-bound signatures on records). Recommended disposition: **fails test 2 as a concern; file as a capability cluster under Structure** — but record the finding that a large fraction of transplantable authority mechanisms are blocked *upstream* by its absence, which makes it high-leverage regardless of taxonomy.
