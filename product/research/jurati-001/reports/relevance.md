## Rationale

### Relevance / target fidelity

The run answered the goal-relevant question as far as its frozen evidence allowed. It tested the evolved
Jurati vision's proposed typed decision-gate kernel across SDLC and research material, built and exercised
the checker/reducer, and subjected the result to an independent firewall gate. It did not drift into a
model leaderboard, a general workflow runtime, a canvas, or an implementation-language choice.

The terminal result is correctly reported as **failed/inconclusive**, not as a negative finding about
Jurati. The approved proof bar required clause agreement, exact verdict and next-action agreement,
false-advancement measurement, and a least-cost reliable judge policy. The frozen references contained
episode verdict/action while predictions contained a synthetic clause result, leaving no scoreable outcome
field in common. Therefore those claims are unavailable rather than failed. Position #264 and `REPORT.md`
preserve that distinction and do not overstate the bounded artifact.

The surviving result is relevant but narrower than the target: technology #263 demonstrates deterministic
reduction after accepted judgment, response/evidence confinement, fail-closed handling, and a recorded local

cross-domain expressibility, false advancement, and exact next-action agreement were not demonstrated.

### Constraint alignment

The synthesis respects the governing constraints:

- the validated firewall remains load-bearing: neither capability nor technology is `proven`;
- the artifact envelope is bounded to what was actually observed, including incomplete B/C execution and
  blocked frontier/human arms;
- historical labels were not retrofitted through a post-hoc mapping after freeze;

- model consistency was not presented as semantic accuracy;
- the independent validator's terminal `SCOPE FAIL` is carried through unchanged.

No secondary constraint was over-weighted into the cause of failure. Cost, frontier availability, human
raters, and local endpoint continuity were real holes, but the decisive defect was the unscoreable
prediction/reference schema.

### Gap / frontier change

The findings expose a capability gap upstream of #256: deterministic consequence cannot establish delivery
quality when the delivery authority can influence its own goal, acceptance bar, evidence, or evaluator.
The broader Jurati vision therefore needs authenticated principals, delegated authority, immutable approved
criteria, non-bypassable enforcement, and independently attributable approvals in addition to the typed
decision kernel.

The merged `jurati-product-authority-control-wedge.md` proposal is an aligned frontier-change direction. It
starts with one product-owned goal and capability roadmap, keeps product/design/delivery authority distinct,
tests Unimatrix-local authorization, a Jurati-mediated hook, issued capabilities, and a hybrid without
preselecting the architecture, and requires real denied mutation attempts with zero unauthorized committed
changes. This advances the evolved vision's authority and sole-path requirements while explicitly avoiding
claims about filesystem, Git, network, secrets, or universal ecosystem mediation.

This follow-on does not complete or replace capability #256. Semantic judgment quality remains unresolved
and should be revisited only with a fresh, smaller corpus whose reference and prediction schemas share
predeclared clause, verdict, and action fields end to end.
