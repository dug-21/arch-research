# Provenance of the sample content (N8)

- **Authored text.** Every sentence of body text in `src/draft.adoc` (foreword, introduction, clauses 1–7,
  terms, notes, tables, figure caption, annex A), every comment in `../task/sheets/*.csv`,
  `../task/invalid/*.csv` and `../task/reviews/**`, and every observation in `../task/dispositions.json` was
  written by `factory-poc` for mnstd-002 on 2026-11-13. The subject is fictitious: *glimmerwood* is an invented
  timber and "ISO/CD 99999-1" is not a real project. Reviewers (alice, bob, claire, dave, erin, mallory) are
  invented. `src/figure1.png` is generated by `tools/mkfigure.py` (two filled shapes).
- **No real standard text.** No clause text is copied from any ISO (or other) standard. The single normative
  reference `GTF 100` is fictitious and marked `nofetch` so no bibliographic lookup happens.
- **Tool-injected boilerplate (declared, Q6, excluded from N8.1/N8.2).** metanorma-iso 3.5.14 inserts, by itself:
  the ISO copyright statement and copyright-office address block (`boilerplate-copyright-default`); the
  "For the purposes of this document…" terms intro and the ISO OBP / IEC Electropedia list in clause 3; the
  normative-references intro sentence ("The following documents are referred to in the text…"); and the
  "Warning for WDs and CDs" draft-stage warning (presentation output). These are the tool's own text, not ours
  (observed in the v1 build of 2026-11-13; no ISO foreword boilerplate was injected in this build).
- **Sources of method, not of text.** The ISO 2012-03 commenting-template column set and the ge/te/ed vocabulary
  come from the public template description (findings-W1 §3.1); field names follow `metanorma/commenter`.
