[[intro]]
== Introduction
Glimmerwood is an invented timber used here only to give the example a subject.
Tuning forks cut from it are imagined to drift in pitch as they dry.

[[cl-scope]]
== Scope
This document specifies a test method for the resonance stability of glimmerwood tuning forks.

It applies to forks with a nominal pitch between 200 Hz and 900 Hz.

[[cl-terms]]
== Terms and definitions

[[term-glimmerwood]]
=== glimmerwood
fictitious timber with a fine, luminous grain

[[term-drift]]
=== resonance drift
change in the fundamental frequency of a fork over a stated conditioning period

[[cl-general]]
== General requirements
Each fork shall be identified by a permanent mark on its handle.

The test shall be carried out on forks that have not been repaired.

[[note-general-1]]
NOTE: A repaired fork can show a drift unrelated to the timber.

[[note-general-2]]
NOTE: Marks made with ink can fade during conditioning.

[[cl-apparatus]]
== Apparatus

[[cl-stand]]
=== Test stand
The test stand holds the fork by its handle without touching the tines.
The recommended dimensions are given in <<tab-stand>>.

[[tab-stand]]
.Test stand dimensions
|===
| Part | Dimension

| Base plate | 200 mm x 150 mm
| Clamp height | 80 mm
|===

Other dimensions may be used if the fork does not touch the base plate.

[[cl-clamp]]
==== Clamp
The clamp shall grip the handle with a soft lining.

The lining shall be replaced when it shows wear.

[[cl-sensor]]
==== Frequency sensor
The frequency sensor shall resolve changes of one tenth of a hertz.

[[cl-mallet]]
=== Mallet
The mallet has a felt head and a wooden shaft.

[[cl-procedure]]
== Procedure

[[cl-conditioning]]
=== Conditioning
Store each fork at the conditions given in <<tab-conditions>> for seven days before the test.

[[tab-conditions]]
.Conditioning environment
|===
| Parameter | Value

| Temperature | 23 degrees Celsius
| Relative humidity | 50 %
|===

[[cl-measurement]]
=== Measurement
Strike the fork with the mallet shown in <<fig-mallet>> and record the fundamental frequency.

Repeat the measurement five times and report the mean.

[[cl-report]]
== Test report
The test report shall include the following information:

* the identification of the fork;
* the conditioning period;
* the mean frequency and the drift.
